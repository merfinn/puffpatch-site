Puff Patch: Picnic Friends

For iPhone and iPad. Hamham the hamster is having a picnic, and her plush friends are coming! Each friend shows a little picture of their wish. Find the treats, give them to your friend and watch them smile.

Coming to the App Store soon

Three islands to explore

Apple Tree

Pick apples, blackberries and tulips, shake the tree and set the picnic blanket. When everyone has their treat, the whole picnic shares a feast.

Bubble Reef

Dive for pearls, starfish, shells and gold coins. Your friends turn them into sparkly jewels for a show on the shell stage.

Dream Soup

In an evening meadow, pour dew drops, moon puffs and star sugar into the pot, stir and serve a dreamy soup.

A new island is on its way. Make every friend happy to open the next island, and finish Bubble Reef for a little piano.

Made for little hands

  • Big picture buttons, no reading needed. Hamham's friendly voice tells you what to do.
  • No timers and no losing: every round ends in a happy party.
  • The Friends book keeps hearts and gifts from every friend.

For grown-ups

No ads, no accounts, no tracking and no data collection. No in-app purchases. Works offline. The grown-ups' corner sits behind a press-and-hold gate, with switches for music, sound, voice and haptics.
